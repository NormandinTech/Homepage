How I work

The brief gets followed the first time.

Most of what goes wrong in subcontracted work is discovered during revisions instead of before the build. This is how I keep that from being your problem.

  1. Questions before the build, not during it

    I read the brief and every supplied asset, then send back one list of gaps, ambiguities, and recommendations. Scope and delivery date get confirmed in writing before I start.

  2. QA against the brief before you see it

    Every requirement checked off, every link and form tested, desktop, tablet, and phone reviewed. What you get for first review is a finished version, not a draft to audit.

  3. Missed spec is on me

    Design revisions are normal and get their own rounds. Anything that doesn't match the original brief gets corrected at no charge and doesn't count against them.

  4. Fixed quotes, named boundaries

    A flat price per project, what's included, and what sits outside scope, all stated up front. Larger work gets phased so you can approve as it goes.
